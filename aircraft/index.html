Aircraft guide

In these Alps, even a trip to Sunday lunch can begin at a grass strip. Meet the aircraft people fly for work, for family visits and for the view.

Luftpost · Village rounds

DHC-2 Beaver

From Bad Ischl, the Beaver delivers letters and parcels to the village strips and brings the outgoing post home.

Luftpost · Regional post

DHC-6 Twin Otter

The Twin Otter carries mail between district depots like Bad Ischl and the regional sorting office in Salzburg.

Luftpost · National post

Airbus A400M Atlas

Two Atlases carry the national mail between Austria’s main sorting offices, flying west from Vienna to Bregenz and south to Klagenfurt.

HÖHENWEG

Airbus A320neo

The A320neo takes passengers sightseeing on the Grand Alpine Traverse, with an observation lounge overlooking the peaks.

Alpine Field Support

Pilatus PC-6 Porter

The ski-equipped Porter flies scientists and supplies into the Sulztal research camp and brings the outgoing crew back to Innsbruck.

Sunrise and St. Notburga

Cessna 152

Mum fits a learner plate to the door of her Cessna, Sunrise, and takes the right seat while Sophie flies them to Sunday lunch at Oma’s.

Feuersalamander

Canadair CL-415

FEUERWACHT fights wildfires from the air with this CL-415, painted in the black and yellow of a fire salamander.
